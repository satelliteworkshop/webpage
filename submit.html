Abstract Submission

Guidelines

You can submit your abstract as a simple text or you can upload a print ready PDF. All abstracts shall follow conference template format and may be up to 2 pages long.

Templates for PDF upload preparation:

  • Text submission has automatic formating (but no images)
  • LaTeX Template for PDF
  • Google Docs Template for PDF

After submission, you will receive an email containing your abstract (as submitted or generated) and an editing link. Keep this email — the same system will later be used to upload your presentation.

Submissions are reviewed by the Program Committee. If accepted, your abstract (max. 2 pages) will be linked to the conference program. No full paper submission is required. Presentation options include a 12+3 minute oral talk or an A1-sized poster during the interactive poster session.
