WSW Space and Defence track 2026

Winter Satellite Workshop organizes a special track for discussing security and defence related topics. The track is targeted mainly to industry and researchers who can discuss current topics and communicate with governmental stakeholders. Thematic topics are divided between fully public Security and Space track and invited Defence and Space track.

Key topics

  • Space contributing security and defence
  • Situational awareness
  • Collaboration and interoperability
  • Innovation and scale-up
